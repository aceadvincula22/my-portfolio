Chapter 02 · My Journey

From playing games to creating them.

I'm a Computer Science student with a growing interest in games and anime.

I've always been fascinated by computers. Long before I wrote any code, I was the kid who wanted to understand how the screen, the keyboard and the machine behind them worked together.

Playing video games since childhood changed the way I look at technology. Every match made me ask questions: how does the game know where I clicked, how are the levels built, and who decided how the characters move? That curiosity slowly turned me from someone who only plays games into someone who wants to know how they are designed, programmed and developed.

Today I'm building that foundation through my Computer Science studies. I'm gaining experience in programming and web development, and I'm creating small projects to practice what I learn. My goal is to keep leveling up until I can develop a video game of my own.
